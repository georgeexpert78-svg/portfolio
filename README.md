# Vehicle History Research Portfolio

## Michael V. George

Automotive data research, vehicle history analysis, and used-car buyer education.

This repository contains selected projects and educational materials focused on vehicle history data, VIN research, title records, mileage analysis, auction history, and used-vehicle risk assessment.

## Areas of Focus

- VIN and vehicle identification research
- Vehicle history analysis
- Title and brand history
- Odometer and mileage review
- Accident and damage history
- Flood-damaged vehicle research
- Auction record analysis
- Used-car buyer education
- Automotive data research

## Portfolio

### Vehicle History Research

Research and educational materials covering how historical vehicle records can help buyers understand the background of a used vehicle.

### VIN Data Projects

Practical experiments and tools related to VIN information and vehicle-history data.

### Used-Car Buyer Guides

Educational resources explaining how to review vehicle history reports, identify warning signs, and prepare for a pre-purchase inspection.
